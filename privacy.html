Privacy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

The short version

Concentric has no accounts, no cookies and no ads. Your edits, progress and settings stay in your own browser.

What is stored on your device

The app uses your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device:

  • concentric-settings: quality, colours, letters, vibration, beat flash, headphone sync.
  • concentric-edit:*: your edited versions of lessons and songs, and your Create sketches.
  • concentric-progress: lessons finished, favourites, recently opened, badges, daily-puzzle results.
  • concentric-last, concentric-last-learn, concentric-last-create, concentric-hint-dot: where you left off, and whether a tip was shown.

To delete all of it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Share links

When you share your version, the notes are packed into the link itself (after #w=). Nothing is uploaded to us. Anyone with the link can open that version.

Usage counts (optional)

If the site owner turns it on, Concentric sends anonymous, cookie-free counts to GoatCounter: page views and events such as “play”, “lesson finished” or “daily puzzle solved”. No personal data, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking. Nothing is sent if your browser’s Do Not Track is on.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Children

Concentric collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
