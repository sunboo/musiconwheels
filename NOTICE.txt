Concentric: credits and notices
================================

Software
--------
- Concentric source code: MIT licence (LICENSE).
- Stylesheet compiled with Tailwind CSS (MIT licence, (c) Tailwind Labs, Inc.).
- No other third-party code, fonts, samples or CDNs are bundled. All sounds are synthesised in the browser.

Music
-----
- Public-domain works: the compositions listed with "public domain" in the app (Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Grieg,
  Bizet, Holst, R. Strauss, Pachelbel, Brahms, traditional and folk melodies, nursery rhymes, hymns).
  Arrangements are our own and simplified for teaching.
- "Happy Birthday to You": melody by Mildred and Patty Hill (1893). Treated as public domain after
  Marya v. Warner/Chappell (C.D. Cal. 2015) and the 2016 settlement.
- Educational arrangements of copyrighted songs are short, transformed, non-commercial study excerpts.
  Rights remain with their owners. Clocks and Billie Jean are playable but cannot be shared or exported.
- National anthems (USA, UK): play-only openings, not editable or remixable.
- Indian taals and ragas (Teentaal, Jhaptaal, Rupak, Keherwa, Dadra, Bhupali, Yaman) are traditional.
  Tabla sounds are synthetic approximations.

- I. Newton, Opticks (1704); A. Scriabin, Prometheus: Poem of Fire (1910): colour and pitch.
