3.2 Chord Inversions (Root, 1st & 2nd)

Same three notes, different bottom note: flipping which note sits on the Outer Bass Ring changes the chord’s weight.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Inversions let pianists and orchestras move smoothly from one chord to the next without jumping wildly across the keyboard—known as smooth voice leading.

Try it

Select any chord (e.g., G Major or C Minor) and switch between Root Position, 1st Inversion, and 2nd Inversion!
