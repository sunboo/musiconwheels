5.4 Rounds & Canons (Frère Jacques)

One melody, started at different times, harmonises with itself.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

In a round (a simple canon) every voice sings the same tune, entering one phrase later. It only works when every phrase fits over every other phrase: here all four phrases sit on a C major chord. On the wheel, a later entry is literally a rotation of the same ring. Pachelbel’s Canon (in Famous Songs) uses the same idea over a repeating bass.

Compare

  • 1 voice: The tune alone: four phrases, each sung twice.
  • 2 voices: Voice 2 starts two bars later: the same ring, rotated.
  • 4 voices: The full four-part round.

Try it

Switch from 1 voice to 2 and 4. Each new ring is the same melody, rotated two bars around the circle.
