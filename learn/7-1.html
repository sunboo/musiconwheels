7.1 Teentaal: 16 Beats in Four Parts

The most common taal in Hindustani music: 16 matras, grouped 4 + 4 + 4 + 4.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

A taal is a rhythmic cycle. Teentaal (“three claps”) has claps on matras 1, 5 and 13 and a wave on 9, the khali (“empty”) section, where the tabla drops its bass strokes (Dha becomes Ta, Dhin becomes Tin). Musicians always know where sam (matra 1) is: improvisations end on it.

Try it

Clap on the X and the numbers, wave on the 0, and say the bols aloud with the tabla.
