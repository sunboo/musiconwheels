5.2 4-Layer Song Stack (Jingle Bells)

See how a complete arrangement layers Bass, Chords, Fast Melody, and Sleigh-Bell Percussion on one wheel.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

James Lord Pierpont published "The One Horse Open Sleigh" in 1857. Notice how the outer bass plays 2 notes per bar while the inner melody plays rapid eighth-note rhythms.

Compare

  • Part 1 · Jingle bells: "Jingle bells, jingle bells, jingle all the way": four bars over C major.
  • Part 2 · Oh what fun: "Oh what fun it is to ride…": F, C, D7 and G7 pull the tune back home.
