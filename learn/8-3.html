8.3 Swing vs. Straight

The same notes. Delay every second eighth note and it starts to swing.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

In straight time, eighth notes split the beat 50/50. In swing they split it long-short, roughly 2:1 (a triplet feel). Jazz, blues and shuffle grooves live here. Swing is not written in the notes: it’s a way of playing them, which is why the dots stay put.

Compare

  • Straight: Even eighths: 50/50.
  • Swing: Long-short: about 62/38.
  • Hard swing: Triplet swing: 2:1, a shuffle.

Try it

Switch between Straight, Swing and Hard swing. Watch the off-beat dots: they don’t move, but they sound later.

Source: Jazz and blues performance practice
