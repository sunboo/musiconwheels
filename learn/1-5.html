1.5 Silence & Negative Space (Rests)

Music is not the notes—it is the silence between them.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

A saying often attributed to Claude Debussy (and to Mozart, with no reliable source for either) holds that music lives in the space between the notes. Whoever said it, strategic rests give melodies suspense and let bass notes ring cleanly.

Compare

  • Sculpted Rests: Empty recesses create dramatic suspense—making every remaining jewel hit twice as hard.
  • Crowded Wall: Every single step is filled—notice how the groove loses drama when nothing is left silent.
