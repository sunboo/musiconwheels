2.3 Steps & Skips (The Major Scale)

Seven carefully spaced steps (W–W–H–W–W–W–H) build the bright rainbow ladder behind countless melodies.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

The Major Scale uses five Whole Steps (2 semitones) and two Half Steps (between E–F and B–C). Those two half-steps create magnetic pull toward the home note.

Try it

Watch the rainbow spectrum climb smoothly from Red (C4) through Orange, Gold, Green, Cyan, Indigo, and Magenta back to C5!
