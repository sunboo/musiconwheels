7.2 Jhaptaal: 10 Beats (2 + 3 + 2 + 3)

Uneven groups of two and three, like a 5/4 that repeats twice.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Jhaptaal’s ten matras are grouped 2 + 3 + 2 + 3. Compare it with Take Five and Holst’s Mars (5/4): the same long-short feel, organised as a cycle.

Try it

Clap on the X and the numbers, wave on the 0, and say the bols aloud with the tabla.

Source: Standard Hindustani theka (traditional)
