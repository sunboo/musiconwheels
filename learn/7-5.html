7.5 Raga Bhupali: Five Notes, Evening Calm

Sa Re Ga Pa Dha: the same five notes as Amazing Grace and Auld Lang Syne.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

A raga is more than a scale: it has its own notes, phrases, mood and time of day. Bhupali uses only Sa Re Ga Pa Dha (no Ma, no Ni): the major pentatonic. It is sung in the early evening. Here Sa = C, over a tanpura drone of Sa and Pa and a Keherwa groove.

Try it

Turn on Sargam to see Sa Re Ga on the dots. Then open Amazing Grace in the Library and compare.
