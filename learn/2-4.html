2.4 Scale Moods (Major vs. Dorian vs. Aeolian)

Lower two notes and Major becomes Dorian; lower one more (the 6th) and Dorian becomes dark Aeolian minor.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Dorian lowers the 3rd and 7th of the Major scale (E → E♭, B → B♭) but keeps the natural 6th (A). Natural minor (Aeolian) also lowers the 6th (A → A♭). That one note is the difference between bittersweet and sad.

Compare

  • Bright C Major: Natural E, A and B: open and optimistic.
  • C Dorian: E♭ and B♭ lowered, but A stays natural: bittersweet, forward-looking.
  • C Aeolian (A♭): Lower the 6th too and the mood turns dark: natural minor.

Try it

Flip between the three scenes and listen to the long note on Beat 3: that 6th is what makes Dorian sound hopeful rather than dark.
