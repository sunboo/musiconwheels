4.1 Musical Gravity (I – IV – V7 – I)

Chords are sentences: Home (I) departs to (IV), builds peak magnetic tension at (V7), and resolves home.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

The V7 → I Authentic Cadence is the strongest gravitational pull in Western music: the leading tone (B) and flat-7th (F) inside G7 both resolve by a single half-step into C Major.

Try it

Watch the harmonic tension peak at Beat 5–6 (G7) and feel the unmistakable sense of arrival when Beat 1 (C Major) returns!
