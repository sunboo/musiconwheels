1.3 Chopping the Beat (4 vs 8 vs 16)

One steady foot tap can hold one slow note, two quick notes, or four rapid ripples at once.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Subdivision is how musicians fit faster melodies over a slow heartbeat without speeding up the tempo. Quarter notes (4), eighth notes (8), and sixteenth notes (16) are pure 1:2:4 ratios.

Try it

Watch all three rings lock to the same 4 beats: Outer = 4 steps, Middle = 8 steps, Inner = 16 steps. Try Soloing each ring in the Layers panel!
