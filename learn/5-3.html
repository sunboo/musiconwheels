5.3 Harmonic Rhythm (Brahms’ Lullaby)

Harmonic rhythm is why chords change slowly once per bar while the melody weaves gentle notes above.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Johannes Brahms composed "Wiegenlied" (Op. 49 No. 4) in 1868 in gentle 3/4 waltz meter. Slow harmonic rhythm creates calm stability—perfect for a lullaby.

Try it

Watch the Outer Bass & Middle Piano move only once every 3 beats (1 bar), while the Inner Melody sings 6 steps per bar!

Source: Johannes Brahms · Wiegenlied, Op. 49 No. 4 (1868)
