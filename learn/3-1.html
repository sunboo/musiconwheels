3.1 Unison vs. Major vs. Minor

Lowering the middle note of a triad by just one semitone shifts a chord from bright daylight to deep reflection.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

A Major triad stacks Root + 4 semitones + 7 semitones. A Minor triad lowers that middle 3rd to 3 semitones above the root—one tiny key change with huge emotional power.

Try it

Pick any Root Note (C, D, E, F, G, A, B) below and watch Beat 1 play Single Unison, Beat 2 play Major, and Beat 3 play Minor!
