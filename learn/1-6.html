1.6 Asymmetrical Grooves (3 + 3 + 2)

Slicing 8 steps unevenly into 3 + 3 + 2 creates the magnetic pull behind modern pop and Latin rhythms.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Known globally as the "Tresillo" pulse, grouping 8 eighth-notes into 3 + 3 + 2 drives Coldplay’s "Clocks", Ed Sheeran’s "Shape of You", and Afro-Cuban son.

Try it

Watch the middle piano & inner marimba strike on steps 1, 4, and 7 (3 + 3 + 2) against the steady 4-beat bass!

Source: Tresillo / Clocks (3+3+2 Syncopation)
