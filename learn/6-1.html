6.1 3-Against-4 Polyrhythm

Two conflicting pulses—a 4-corner square and a 3-point triangle—share one circle and reunite at Beat 1.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Polyrhythms ("many rhythms") are foundational in West African drumming and Chopin’s piano works. Say "Pass the gold-en but-ter" evenly to hear 3-against-4.

Try it

Watch the Outer Ring strike 4 equal steps while the Inner Ring strikes 3 equal steps across the exact same lap!
