4.4 Four Cadences: How Phrases End

A cadence is musical punctuation: a full stop, an “Amen”, a plot twist or a comma.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Authentic cadence (V → I) is a full stop. Plagal cadence (IV → I) is the “Amen” at the end of hymns. Deceptive cadence (V → vi) sets up home and then swerves to a minor chord. Half cadence (… → V) stops on V, like a comma or a question.

Compare

  • Authentic: V → I: the full stop.
  • Plagal: IV → I: the “Amen”.
  • Deceptive: V → vi: a plot twist.
  • Half: … → V: a comma, or a question.
