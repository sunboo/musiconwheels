1.1 The Heartbeat (Pulse & BPM)

Before melody or harmony exist, music begins with an unbroken human pulse.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Tempo (BPM — Beats Per Minute) measures the speed of your foot tap, not the number of notes. At 60 BPM, every beat is one second; at 120 BPM, the pulse doubles.

Try it

Tap the center TAP Heart jewel to set your own tempo, or toggle off Beat 3 to feel what happens when a pulse drops out.

Source: Universal Metronome & Maelzel Pulse Scale
