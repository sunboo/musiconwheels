Concentric · Famous Songs

1. Canon in D — Johann Pachelbel (c. 1680)

Eight chords that loop forever: the original "four-chord song", 300 years early.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Pachelbel builds the whole piece over a repeating 8-note bass (a "ground bass"). The chord pattern D–A–Bm–F♯m–G–D–G–A still powers pop songs today.

Try it

Watch the outer bass walk the same 8 notes each lap while the inner rings weave around it.
