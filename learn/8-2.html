8.2 Pentatonic & Blues Scales

Five notes that never clash, and the one “blue note” that makes them cry.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

The major pentatonic (C D E G A) leaves out the two notes that create half-steps, so nothing clashes: it is found from Scottish folk to Raga Bhupali. The minor pentatonic (C E♭ F G B♭) is the backbone of blues and rock solos. Add the flat 5th (F♯/G♭) and you get the blues scale.

Compare

  • Major pentatonic: C D E G A: bright, open, no clashes.
  • Minor pentatonic: C E♭ F G B♭: the rock and blues solo scale.
  • Blues scale: Minor pentatonic plus the blue note F♯.

Try it
