1.7 Faster & Louder (Accelerando & Crescendo)

Grieg repeats one phrase over and over. The drama comes from tempo and volume growing every time.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Accelerando means gradually getting faster; crescendo means gradually getting louder. In "In the Hall of the Mountain King" (1875) the melody barely changes: the orchestra speeds up and adds instruments until it explodes. Here each loop adds 6 BPM, and the rings enter one by one.

Try it

Press Play and leave it: each loop is faster and adds a ring. Press Stop to reset. Remix → turn off "Faster each loop" to compare.

Source: E. Grieg, Peer Gynt Suite No. 1 (1875) · public domain
