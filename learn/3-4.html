3.4 Block Chords vs. Arpeggios

An arpeggio takes a stacked chord and unrolls its notes clockwise around the circle like ripples on water.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

From Beethoven’s "Moonlight Sonata" to Coldplay’s "Clocks", arpeggios turn static harmony into flowing rhythmic motion.

Try it

Pick any chord below and flip between "Block Chord" (all notes at once) and "Arpeggio Wave" (notes rippling across Beats 1–4)!
