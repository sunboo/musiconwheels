4.2 The 4-Chord Loop (I – V – vi – IV)

Four chords—three bright Major, one emotional Minor—power hundreds of unforgettable modern anthems.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

By placing the relative minor (vi) right after V, the progression avoids ending too early and creates an endless, uplifting emotional loop ("Let It Be", "No Woman No Cry", "Someone Like You").

Try it

Listen for the poignant emotional dip at Beat 5 when the progression lands on A Minor (vi)!
