7.6 Raga Yaman: The Sharp Ma

Yaman raises Ma (F → F♯): one changed note gives a bright, yearning colour.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Yaman uses all seven notes, but its Ma is tivra (sharp): Sa Re Ga Ma♯ Pa Dha Ni. Its ascent usually starts from the Ni below Sa (N R G M♯ D N S′), skipping Pa. It is among the first ragas students learn and is sung after sunset. In Western terms it is the Lydian mode.

Try it

Watch for the F♯ dot (tivra Ma). Edit it to F and hear the raga lose its character.

Source: Raga Yaman (Hindustani tradition)
