2. Jingle Bells — J. L. Pierpont (1857)

The chorus in two halves: four bars on C, then the F–C–D7–G7 turnaround.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Published in 1857 as "The One Horse Open Sleigh". Part 1 stays on one chord while the melody repeats E; Part 2 moves through F, C, D7 and G7 to build the pull back home.

Compare

  • Part 1 · Jingle bells: "Jingle bells, jingle bells, jingle all the way": four bars over C major.
  • Part 2 · Oh what fun: "Oh what fun it is to ride…": F, C, D7 and G7 pull the tune back home.

Try it

Open Song Story and flip between Part 1 and Part 2. Mute the bells to hear the dotted "all the way" rhythm.
