1.4 Accents & Ghost Notes

Playing the exact same note with large accents and tiny ghost notes turns a robot into a drummer.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Funk and R&B drummers play quiet "ghost notes" on the snare between the loud backbeats on 2 and 4. You barely hear them one by one, but together they create forward momentum without overpowering the backbeat. Accents are the loud notes; ghost notes are the whispers.

Compare

  • Human Groove: Large dots punch Beats 1 & 3; medium dots snap Beats 2 & 4; tiny dots whisper in between.
  • Flat Machine: Every dot is identical in size and volume—notice how quickly the groove loses its bounce.
