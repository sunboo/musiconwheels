4. Twinkle, Twinkle — Traditional · Variations by Mozart, K. 265

An 8-beat question-and-answer melody that leaps a Perfect 5th (C → G) before stepping home.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

The tune is the French folk song "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman". Mozart wrote twelve variations on it (K. 265) in Vienna around 1781–82. The harmony is a clean I–IV–I–V7–I.

Compare

  • Theme: The plain theme: block chords under the melody.
  • Alberti-bass variation: A variation in the style of Mozart’s era: each chord is broken into a low–high–middle–high "Alberti" pattern.
