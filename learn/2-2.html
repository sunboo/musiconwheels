2.2 Tension vs. Heroism (Half-step vs. Perfect 5th)

One half-step creates the looming dread of a shark-movie bass line; a perfect 5th launches the sunrise heroism of Also sprach Zarathustra.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

A Minor 2nd (1 semitone apart) causes sound waves to rub closely together, creating suspense. A Perfect 5th (7 semitones apart, a 3:2 frequency ratio) locks into pure acoustic resonance.

Compare

  • 1. Half-step (Tension): Half-step friction: E2 creeps up just ONE tiny half-step to F2 on Beat 2.
  • 2. Zarathustra (Heroism): Also sprach Zarathustra (Strauss, 1896): C leaps a Perfect 5th up to G, then up to the octave C.

Try it

Flip between "1. Half-step (Tension)" and "2. Zarathustra (Heroism)" so you hear and see one crystal-clear interval at a time.
