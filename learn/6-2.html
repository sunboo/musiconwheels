6.2 Rotation: Son Clave & Tresillo

The 3-2 son clave is five hits on a 16-step circle. Rotate it half a turn and it becomes 2-3 clave.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

The son clave (Cuba) places five hits in 16 sixteenth-notes: 3 on one side, 2 on the other. The tresillo (3 + 3 + 2) shares three of them and runs under salsa, habanera, reggaeton and much of pop. Both are “maximally even”: the hits spread as evenly around the circle as their number allows, which is why they feel balanced and propulsive at once.

Try it

Use “Rotate clave” in the guide. One step shifts the feel; 8 steps (half the circle) turns 3-2 clave into 2-3 clave.

Source: Afro-Cuban son clave (traditional) · G. Toussaint, The Geometry of Musical Rhythm (2013)
