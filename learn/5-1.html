5.1 Call & Response (Twinkle, Twinkle)

Great melodies converse like human speech: Beats 1–4 ask a musical question, and Beats 5–8 answer it home.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Mozart composed twelve famous variations on this exact 18th-century French melody ("Ah! vous dirai-je, maman") because its balanced 4+4 bar question-and-answer symmetry is perfection.

Compare

  • Theme: The plain theme: block chords under the melody.
  • Alberti-bass variation: A variation in the style of Mozart’s era: each chord is broken into a low–high–middle–high "Alberti" pattern.
