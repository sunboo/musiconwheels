2.6 Three Minor Scales (Natural, Harmonic, Melodic)

Raise one note of the minor scale and the V chord suddenly pulls home.

▶ Open it on the circle

Why it works

Natural minor (A B C D E F G) has no leading note: its V chord is E minor, which pulls home only weakly. Harmonic minor raises G to G♯, making an E major chord with a strong pull to A, at the cost of an exotic leap from F to G♯. Melodic minor also raises F to F♯ on the way up, smoothing that leap; classical musicians traditionally use natural minor on the way down.

Compare

  • Natural: A B C D E F G A: the V chord is E minor.
  • Harmonic: Raise G to G♯: the V chord becomes E major.
  • Melodic: Raise F and G on the way up: smooth and strong.
